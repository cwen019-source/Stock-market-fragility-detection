為什麼改版

  1. 同源指標重複計票。舊版 6 燈裡有 3 盞都是融資餘額的變形(彼此相關 0.6–0.87),有效獨立資訊只有約 3 個;「≥4 亮紅」實際上等於「融資偏高 + 再一個」。
  2. 沒有「價格是否有實體支撐」的檢查。舊版只看槓桿與市場情緒,沒有估值、企業盈餘/營收、景氣循環。
  3. 沒有定義要預測什麼、也沒量過準不準。舊規則在 60 天尺度沒有預警力,美股甚至反向。

新架構(每一點對應一個專業作法)

  1. 四類脆弱性,每類一票(Fed 金融穩定報告):V 估值 vs 基本面、L 槓桿與信用、M 景氣循環(實體經濟)、S 市場壓力。同類先平均成一分,再轉成歷史百分位。
  2. 無前視偏誤:只用當時已公布的資料(由期末起算公布落後:Z.1 75 天、GDP 35 天、BIS 160 天、月營收次月 10 日),再轉成 PIT 擴張百分位。獨立審查後修正了 5 處前視問題。
  3. 相關性感知的綜合分數(ECB CISS):(w∘s)ᵀ · Cₜ · (w∘s),Cₜ 為四類 EWMA 時變相關(λ=0.94),四類同時偏高才會高。
  4. 紅區(Greenwood-Hanson-Shleifer-Sørensen 2022):信用 3 年成長前 20% 且股價 3 年漲幅前 1/3。
  5. Growth-at-Risk(IMF):分量迴歸估計未來 12 個月報酬第 10 百分位,只用當時已實現樣本重估。
  6. 事前定義的等級:0 平穩;1 留意(綜合 ≥80 或任一類 ≥90);2 結構脆弱(紅區,或 ≥2 類 ≥80 且含估值/槓桿);3 主動減碼(等級 ≥2 且快速觸發)。
  7. 快速觸發(Fed 區分脆弱性與衝擊):跌破長期均線(美股 200 日、台股 240 日)或 VIX 5 日漲幅 ≥85 百分位。
  8. 訊號法評估(Kaminsky-Lizondo-Reinhart):事件 = 12 個月內比當時價位低 ≥20%;報告命中率、假警報、NSR、bootstrap 信賴區間,並與舊版同期比較。

警報信:等級升到 ≥2 寄出;維持 ≥2 每週一提醒;等級 3(主動減碼)每天寄;降回 <2 寄解除。

限制(請務必一起看)

  • 熊市次數少(美股 1960 年後 10 次、台股 2007 年後 5 次),月樣本視窗重疊 → 統計差距是方向性證據,請看信賴區間。
  • 台股估值與營收使用目前前 25 大權值股(存活者偏誤);台股無免費的長期全市場本益比與景氣燈號資料。
  • 台灣 GDP 取自 IMF WEO 最新版本(含事後修正);美國季資料亦為修正後數值,只以公布落後近似 PIT。
  • 台股景氣類(營收)沒有預警力;美股市場壓力類偏同時指標。兩者保留、權重不變,避免依回測挑指標。
  • 美股長期日線來自 Yahoo Finance 非官方介面(有快取與種子備援,近期以 FRED 補齊);CAPE 來自 multpl.com。
  • 結構脆弱度描述的是未來 1–2 年的尾端風險,不是短線擇時工具。非投資建議。

  1. Duplicate signals. Three of the old six lights were variants of margin debt (correlation 0.6–0.87), so the six lights carried only ~3 independent signals; "≥4 red" effectively meant "margin is high, plus one more".
  2. No check on whether prices are backed by the real economy. The old version looked only at leverage and market sentiment — no valuation, earnings/revenue or business cycle.
  3. No defined target and no measured accuracy. The old rule had no warning power at a 60-day horizon, and in the US it even pointed the wrong way.

New design (each part follows a professional method)

  1. Four vulnerability categories, one vote each (Fed Financial Stability Report): V valuation vs fundamentals, L leverage & credit, M business cycle (real economy), S market stress. Indicators within a category are averaged, then converted to a historical percentile.
  2. No look-ahead: only data published by each date (lags counted from period end: Z.1 75 days, GDP 35, BIS 160, monthly revenue by the 10th of the next month), converted to point-in-time expanding percentiles. An independent review found and fixed 5 look-ahead issues.
  3. Correlation-aware composite (ECB CISS): (w∘s)ᵀ · Cₜ · (w∘s) with Cₜ the EWMA time-varying correlation (λ=0.94) — high only when categories are elevated together.
  4. Red zone (Greenwood-Hanson-Shleifer-Sørensen 2022): 3-year credit growth in the top 20% AND 3-year price gain in the top third.
  5. Growth-at-Risk (IMF): quantile regression for the 10th-percentile 12-month return, re-estimated each month on outcomes already realised.
  6. Levels fixed in advance: 0 Calm; 1 Watch (composite ≥80 or any category ≥90); 2 Vulnerable (red zone, or ≥2 categories ≥80 incl. valuation/leverage); 3 Reduce longs (level ≥2 AND fast trigger).
  7. Fast trigger (Fed: vulnerabilities vs shocks): close below the long-term moving average (US 200-day, Taiwan 240-day) or VIX 5-day change ≥ 85th percentile.
  8. Signal-approach evaluation (Kaminsky-Lizondo-Reinhart): event = price ≥20% below the signal-date level within 12 months; hit rate, false alarms, NSR and bootstrap confidence intervals, compared with the old rule over the same period.

Alert emails: sent when the level rises to ≥2; weekly Monday reminder while ≥2; daily at level 3 (reduce longs); an all-clear when it drops below 2.

Limitations (please read)

  • Few bear markets (10 in the US since 1960, 5 in Taiwan since 2007) and overlapping monthly windows → treat differences as directional evidence and check the confidence intervals.
  • Taiwan valuation and revenue use today's 25 largest companies (survivorship bias); no free long-history market-wide P/E or business-cycle indicator exists for Taiwan.
  • Taiwan GDP comes from the latest IMF WEO vintage (includes later revisions); US quarterly data are also revised values, with publication lags approximating point-in-time.
  • Taiwan's business-cycle category (revenue) shows no warning power; US market stress is mostly coincident. Both are kept at equal weight to avoid picking indicators by backtest.
  • Long US daily prices come from an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint (cached, seeded, and topped up from FRED); CAPE from multpl.com.
  • Structural fragility describes tail risk over the next 1–2 years; it is not a short-term timing tool. Not investment advice.
